What it does

  • NowCPU, memory, GPU, disk, network and battery, with the app behind each.
  • AppsProcesses grouped into the apps that launched them. Quit or force quit.
  • TodayA short written summary of the day.
  • Time MachineGo back to any minute and see what was running.
  • Disk CleanupDeveloper caches and old build files, moved to the Trash.
  • Claude CodeSessions, search, token usage and plan limits.
  • Ports and DockerLocal servers and containers.
  • Menu barReadouts in the menu bar, and a ⌘K palette.

No account, no analytics, no crash reports. It only goes online to check for updates and to ask Anthropic for your own Claude plan limits. Privacy
